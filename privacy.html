Rotation Planner

Privacy Policy

Last updated October 19, 2026

The short version

  • Rotation Planner is a free planner made by a Bolles student for Bolles students. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by The Bolles School, Schoology or PowerSchool.
  • Without an account, everything stays in your browser on your device.
  • With an account, your planner is saved privately so it works on your phone and laptop. Only you can see your assignments, grades and reminders.
  • Friends you approve can see your name and class schedule, never your assignments, grades or email.
  • No ads, no analytics or tracking, and your information is never sold or shared with anyone else.
  • You can erase your data at any time.

What the planner stores

Without an accountYour school and grade, classes (names, teachers, rooms, periods), assignments, activities, study plan and settings. These are saved only in your browser on that device and never leave it.
With an accountYour email address and password (the password is stored only in scrambled, hashed form by our sign-in provider), plus the same planner information, saved to your private account so it syncs between your devices.
Schoology calendar linkIf you add your Schoology calendar link, it is saved in your planner. When the planner syncs it, the link is fetched through our server and the calendar goes straight back to you; the server keeps no copy.
Grades (browser extension)If you install the Rotation Planner Grades extension, it reads your own Schoology grades page in your browser while you are logged in to Schoology, the same page you can already see. It never sees your Schoology or Microsoft password. Your grades are saved in your planner and, if you have an account, in your private account so your phone shows them too. The extension uses a private key that can only save grades to your account; only a scrambled (hashed) version of that key is stored.
NotificationsIf you turn on notifications, we store the address your browser gives us for sending to that device, and the list of your upcoming reminders (for example "Test tomorrow: AP Biology"). Reminders are deleted after they are sent, within about 40 days. Messages are encrypted so that only your device can read them.
FriendsIf you turn on Friends, we store the display name you choose, a private friend code, your friend requests, and your schedule (campus, grade, and each class's period, name, teacher, room and lunch). Only friends you approve can see it. There is no way to search for students. Your invite link contains your friend code and first name, so share it only with people you'd like to add you; you still approve every request.
Connect Schoology (if offered)If a future version lets you connect your Schoology account directly, you would approve it on Schoology's own website. The planner would only read your own courses, assignments and grades, never change anything in Schoology, and store the access permission in your private account. You could disconnect at any time.

Who can see your information

  • You. Every row in the database is locked to its owner, so other students cannot read your planner, grades or reminders.
  • Friends you approve see only your name and schedule, as described above.
  • The person who runs the planner. As the developer, I have administrator access to the database. I don't look at individual students' information, except to fix a problem you ask me to help with.
  • Nobody else. Your information is never sold, rented or shared for advertising, and it is not shared with the school.

Services the planner uses

To work, the planner relies on a few outside services. Each only gets what it needs:

  • Supabase stores accounts and saved planners and runs our small server functions (United States).
  • GitHub Pages hosts the website.
  • jsDelivr delivers the sign-in code library to your browser.
  • Gmail sends account emails (confirming your email, resetting your password) from bollesplanner@gmail.com.
  • Your browser's push service (Google, Apple, Microsoft or Mozilla) delivers notifications. Messages are encrypted, so these services cannot read them.
  • Schoology, only when you use the calendar link or the extension, and only to read your own information.

Like any website, these services may see your IP address and basic technical information when your browser connects to them.

Deleting your information

  • Everything in the planner: Settings → About → Erase all planner data. When you are signed in, this erases your planner, grades, reminders and friends profile from your account, as well as from that device.
  • Just your grades: Settings → Grades → Remove grades from my planner. This also stops the extension from sending grades to your account.
  • Friends: Friends → Stop sharing and remove all friends.
  • Notifications: Settings → Notifications → Turn off on this device.
  • Your whole account, including your email address: email bollesplanner@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, and it will be deleted within 30 days.
  • The extension: removing it from your browser deletes what it stored there.

Students under 13

The planner works fully without an account, and nothing leaves your device that way. If you are under 13, please ask a parent before creating an account, using the extension's account sync, or turning on Friends or notifications.

Security

All connections use encryption (HTTPS). Accounts use our provider's secure sign-in, and database rules make sure each student can only reach their own information. No system is perfect; if something ever went wrong that affected your information, you would be told by email.

Changes and questions

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change, and important changes will be announced in the planner. Questions or requests: bollesplanner@gmail.com.
